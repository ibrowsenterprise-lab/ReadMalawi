Publishing rules

Anyone can submit a book privately. Only original, licensed or otherwise authorised works can be made public. For unknown rights, select 'Unsure' and a reviewer can investigate.

Possessing a copy does not establish permission to publish it. Submission is not automatic public distribution.

Future sustainability

Keep reading affordable and accessible

Download support plan · Proposed

5 free ReadMalawi-hosted downloads per eligible account, followed by an optional MK500 access pass once verified payments and rights-compliant access are ready.

The period, allowance and exceptions still require approval. Public free-source links are never included in a paid download count. No payment is being collected on this site.

Support ReadMalawi · Planned

Proposed voluntary donations from MK100, to support learning resources and future book outreach once an authorised fundraising arrangement is established.

Financial donations are not currently accepted here. Volunteer time, translation reviews and rights-cleared original books are welcome forms of support.

Public viewing does not technically prevent a reader from saving a digital file. A future paid-download service therefore needs a carefully designed reader, permissions model and server-side payment enforcement—not merely a browser counter.
