Reader information · 15 October 2026

Your reading, your choices.

Reading without an account

Browsing and reading released books do not require email sign-in. The site’s hosting and storage providers receive normal connection requests when pages and books load. This page does not claim anonymous network access.

Personal files

My Reading Space opens your chosen PDF, EPUB or text file in your browser. It does not upload the file to ReadMalawi, send it to an AI provider, or publish it. PDF and EPUB tools are downloaded from jsDelivr when required. Close & clear removes the active book from the page; reloading also discards it.

Save my place stores a file identifier and page number in this browser, not the book contents. Clear ReadMalawi’s site data in your browser to remove saved positions. People sharing your device may be able to see locally saved settings or an open book.

Read-aloud voices

Speech is provided by your browser or operating system. Some available voices use an online speech service. The voice selector labels device and online services where the browser identifies them. ReadMalawi cannot promise every device voice operates offline. No voice selection translates a book.

Email sign-in, comments and enquiries

Email sign-in is handled through Supabase. Comments submitted for publication are reviewed by librarians; use a suitable public display name and do not include private information. Partnership and programme enquiry details are sent to ReadMalawi for private administrative follow-up. Do not include school pupil names, account numbers, passwords or identity documents.

To request correction or deletion of information you submitted, email info@readmalawi.fyi. Identify the submission without sending unnecessary sensitive records.

Study tools and AI

Smart Study Sprint processes your notes in the current browser tab. Its routine and keyword prompts are not generative AI feedback. Guided Reading Buddy searches catalogue information and provides site help. Cloud AI translation and narration are not currently available. If a future cloud AI option is enabled, its data-sharing information must be shown before you use it.

Children and classroom use

Children should use the site with appropriate parent or teacher guidance. Adults should coordinate any external WhatsApp contact or programme enquiry. Do not post children’s contact information or private educational records. Teachers remain responsible for assessing material and supervising classroom activities.

Content and accessibility concerns

Opening a personal file does not establish permission to republish it. Community publication requires separate librarian review. If you own rights to a listed work or find an accessibility, accuracy or safety issue, contact info@readmalawi.fyi with the title or page and your concern.

Contributions and optional recognition

Donors remain anonymous publicly by default. After confirming receipt, we obtain explicit permission before publishing a chosen supporter name, and separate permission before publishing an amount. Phone numbers, account details and transaction references are not published.

ReadMalawi uses private transaction details to confirm and reconcile contributions. Public anonymity does not hide the transaction from the receiving bank or mobile service. Public financial summaries use aggregate figures.

To change your recognition preference or request removal from ReadMalawi’s supporter acknowledgements, email info@readmalawi.fyi. Removing a public acknowledgement does not erase transaction records needed for reconciliation. Contribution information →

Private suggestions and website feedback

The private feedback form requires no name, email or sign-in. Its submissions and administrator notes are readable only by authorised ReadMalawi administrators through the website’s access controls. They are not published in a public ideas feed, including after review. Separate permission is required before sharing a member’s words elsewhere.

Do not include sensitive personal or payment information. Without contact details, we cannot send an individual reply; email info@readmalawi.fyi when you need a response. Ordinary connection requests still reach our hosting and database providers.
