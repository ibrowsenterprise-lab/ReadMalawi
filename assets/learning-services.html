    How to use your guide

    Think, don't just copy.

    • Read the passage once to understand the subject.
    • Close the passage and try explaining the main idea aloud.
    • Use your keyword prompts to test what you remember.
    • Check your understanding against the source or with a teacher.
    This tool provides a routine and keyword prompts. It does not grade your understanding. Check your summary against your notes or with a teacher.

    Our sustainability approach

    Books bring readers. Learning services help sustain access.

    Free original stories, public-source links and community reading stay open. Potential revenue comes from contracted workshops, institutional services, sponsorships and future optional learning features—not charging readers simply to follow publicly available book links.
