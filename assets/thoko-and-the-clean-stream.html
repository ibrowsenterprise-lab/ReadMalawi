Thoko and the Clean Stream

A short fictional story written for ReadMalawi · English

Thoko and her brother walked home from school along a path in their village. Beyond the path flowed a small stream.

“Look,” said Thoko. “There are plastic bags beside the water.”

Her brother bent down to pick one up, but Thoko stopped him. “The bank is slippery. Let's ask an adult before we go near it.”

At home they told their aunt what they had seen. Their aunt spoke to neighbours, who planned a safe clean-up of the path.

On Saturday, the adults brought gloves, sacks and strong shoes. They kept children away from the stream and collected litter only from safe, dry ground.

Thoko and her brother helped in another way. They drew signs that said, “Keep our paths clean. Put rubbish in a bin.”

After the clean-up, Thoko saw that the path looked brighter. “A small idea can help,” she said.

“Yes,” said her aunt. “And the best way to help is to work together and stay safe.”
