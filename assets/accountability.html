A reading community, growing carefully.

ReadMalawi is a founder-led digital reading initiative in Malawi. Our work today is digital book sharing, an online library and original reading activities. We are not currently a registered NGO or charity.

Who is responsible?

Jones Nalikungwi, founder, is the public contact for the initiative and partnership enquiries. Contact info@readmalawi.fyi, call +265 882 242 594, or write to P.O. Box 30415, Blantyre, Malawi.

ReadMalawi is presented here as an independent initiative. This website does not establish that IBROWS Enterprise, a school or another organisation is its contracting entity, fiscal sponsor or financial guarantor. The receiving and signing entity must be identified and agreed in writing before any funding or service arrangement.

What exists today

  • An online catalogue of released books, with a live count on the library page.
  • Ten original English stories for children and beginners, with simple comprehension activities.
  • A WhatsApp reading community for recommendations and digital sharing.
  • Private book opening and device read-aloud, plus a browser-based study routine.

The catalogue count describes available records. It is not a count of active readers, completed books or improved literacy. The larger import archive is not counted as published reading material.

What has not been claimed as completed

We have not completed physical book collection or distribution to schools or prisons. Institutional reading programmes and AI workshops are available for discussion, not established delivery results. The 2019 Nation coverage records the early idea and discussions with DD Phiri; it is historical context, not evidence of delivered outcomes.

Our immediate priorities

  1. Review catalogue titles, authors, subject labels and publication-permission records.
  2. Improve reliable mobile reading and accessibility.
  3. Develop a small English–Chichewa collection with fluent review.
  4. Agree a small teacher-supervised reading pilot before expanding to more institutions.

For schools: a sample session to discuss

Proposed format, not an active booking: one 45-minute teacher-supervised session using an original ReadMalawi story. Reading level, group size, language, facilitator and device/data availability are agreed with the school first.

  1. 5 minutes: introduce the topic and explain the activity.
  2. 15 minutes: individual or shared reading, with vocabulary support.
  3. 15 minutes: discuss the main idea and answer comprehension questions.
  4. 10 minutes: reflect and complete a short teacher-led check.

Possible objectives: identify the main idea, explain a new word in context and support an answer using the text. These are proposed objectives, not guaranteed results. The school retains supervision; learner names and sensitive records should not be submitted in the enquiry form. Facilitator credentials, safeguarding responsibilities, fees and evaluation must be agreed before delivery.
