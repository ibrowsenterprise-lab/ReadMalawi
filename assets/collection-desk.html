Collection & school/prison handover guide (draft) ▾

This is an internal procedure to prepare for a future authorised book drive. It is not permission to schedule real collection or distribution.

  1. Receive the offer: Donors provide only a rough count and general area on WhatsApp. Thank them without promising a pickup.
  2. Plan privately: Once authorised, a coordinator checks staffing, routes and storage capacity. Any detailed collection address stays in a one-to-one WhatsApp conversation.
  3. Receive safely: For a future live drive, count physical books and check their condition. Keep a real inventory/receipt separate from TEST offers.
  4. Agree recipient permissions: Coordinate with school leadership or the responsible prison authority before any handover.
  5. Record only verified impact: Offered books, collected books and distributed books are different figures and must never be conflated.
