Catalogue hundreds of PDFs in one batch

Select your PDF files or a folder on your computer. This tool reads existing PDF metadata and first-page text, drafts titles, authors, ISBNs, subjects and languages, then creates a spreadsheet. No typing each book.

Step 1 — optional private scan: analyse PDF metadata on your own computer and download the CSV without uploading any files. Scanning alone never uploads a file. Ownership of a PDF is not proof of publication rights.

Step 2 · Private bulk upload

Choose your PDF files or folder above, then return here. This uses that same selection. You may analyse the files first for better titles, or upload without scanning (filenames will become draft titles). Every submission stays private and pending until a librarian checks publication rights.

For authorised collections only. Bulk uploading does not grant ReadMalawi distribution rights. Do not select confidential company documents, pirated books, personal records, or materials you do not have permission to submit. The librarian must separately verify rights before any publication. File limit: 50 MB each. Each click processes up to 25 PDFs or 250 MB; click again to continue with the next batch. Do not close this tab while a batch is running.
Checking librarian sign-in…
Sign in on ReadMalawi
Sign in with an authorised librarian account, choose a folder, tick the confirmation, then begin. Files are uploaded sequentially to limit memory use. Re-select the same folder to resume after an interruption; content hashes prevent duplicate batch submissions.

The browser must remain open during transfer. A PDF already in the private storage may be skipped or its missing catalogue entry recovered. This first bulk upload version handles PDFs; EPUB and audiobook bulk import will follow separate validation.

ReadMalawi · Local scanning and private bulk upload are separate, voluntary actions. Upload does not mean public publication, and no payments are collected.
