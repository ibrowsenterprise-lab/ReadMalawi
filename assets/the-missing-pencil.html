The Missing Pencil

A free fictional story written for ReadMalawi · English

Kamuzu had one favourite pencil. It was yellow, with a tiny blue star near the top. He sharpened it every morning before writing.

During a drawing lesson, the pencil vanished. Kamuzu looked under his book, inside his bag and beside his chair.

“Someone must have taken it,” he whispered to his friend Ruth.

Ruth looked around the classroom. “Maybe,” she said, “but we do not know that. Let's search before we blame anyone.”

They checked the shelf where the crayons were kept. They checked the floor near the windows. The pencil was nowhere to be seen.

Then Ruth noticed a small blue star under the edge of a desk. The pencil had rolled behind a wooden leg.

Kamuzu reached for it and laughed. “I thought I had lost it forever.”

He remembered what he had said about someone taking it. His face grew warm. “I was about to accuse somebody without knowing,” he admitted.

“Finding out is better than guessing,” Ruth said.

Kamuzu thanked her. He put his pencil in a small case so it would not roll away again.

When the teacher asked what they had learned that morning, Kamuzu raised his hand. “Sometimes the answer is hiding under a desk,” he said. “And sometimes we need to ask better questions.”
