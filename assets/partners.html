Current status: ReadMalawi is an independent digital reading initiative, not yet a registered NGO or charity. Voluntary contributions support hosting, library maintenance, reading accessibility and reviewed local-language materials. They are separate from book access and paid services. Contributions start at MK700 through TNM Mpamba or FCB; see support information. This enquiry form accepts expressions of interest only. Temporary contribution instructions are on the support page. We do not promise tax-deductible donations, sell shares, or guarantee returns. Any fundraising or investment would require suitable legal and financial arrangements.
