The Rainy Day Book Club

A short fictional story written for ReadMalawi · English

Rain tapped softly on the roof of a classroom in Blantyre. Four friends were waiting for the rain to stop.

Chikondi pulled a storybook from her bag. “We could start a book club,” she said.

“What's a book club?” asked Peter.

“We read a story, then talk about it,” Chikondi explained. “Everyone can share an idea.”

Thandi took the first turn. She read about a bird that was afraid to fly. When she finished, the friends talked about times they had felt afraid.

Peter sat quietly. “I don't like reading aloud,” he whispered. “Sometimes I get the words wrong.”

“You can listen,” said Thandi. “Or you can read just one sentence when you're ready. Both are fine.”

Peter smiled. He pointed to a picture in the book. “I think the bird is brave because it tries again.”

“That's a wonderful thought,” Chikondi said. The friends agreed to meet again next week. Outside, the rain slowed to a gentle drip.
