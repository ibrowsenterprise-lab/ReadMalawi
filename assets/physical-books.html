Tiyeni tiwelenge · Community book giving

Have books to give? We'll help you get started.

No lists of titles. No authors. No lengthy forms. Just tell us approximately how many physical books you have and your general location.

Offer your physical books

Two quick details, then share your offer directly with the ReadMalawi community on WhatsApp. A coordinator can discuss whether collection is possible.

How it works: The button opens a prewritten WhatsApp message. Choose the ReadMalawi group or a coordinator's chat and tap Send. We don't store these two details on the website. Collection is arranged separately through WhatsApp if our team and volunteers are available.

What will happen to the books?

We hope to build a community collection programme that can eventually support schools and prisons. Suitable recipients, collection routes, consent and any prison permissions must be agreed before distribution.

An offer is not yet a completed collection or a guaranteed pickup. No fees or financial donations are requested.

Prefer to read and share digitally?

Our existing WhatsApp reading forum lets members ask for books, exchange recommendations and point each other towards lawful reading sources.

Please respect authors' and publishers' rights. Only share books that may legally be shared.
