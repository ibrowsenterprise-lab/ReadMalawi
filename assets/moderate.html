Bulk publication (paused)

Publish many books in one action when every selected work has been checked and ReadMalawi has documented permission to make it public. This is a librarian-only action. Publications become publicly readable immediately, while downloads remain disabled.

Important: One permission basis and one supporting evidence reference must apply to ALL selected books in the batch. If books have different licences or owners, approve separate groups. Test documents and obvious vacancies/company/government notices cannot be published in bulk.
