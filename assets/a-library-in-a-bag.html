A Library in a Bag

A free fictional story written for ReadMalawi · English

On Monday morning, a teacher called Mrs Banda arrived at school with a large cloth bag. It looked much too heavy for exercise books.

“What is inside?” asked Chisomo. “A surprise,” said Mrs Banda. She placed the bag on a desk and opened it slowly. Out came a picture book about a dog, a book of poems, a story about a clever child and a thin book about stars.

“We do not have a room full of books,” the teacher said, “but we can still have a library. We can start with a bag.”

Each pupil chose a story to read or look through. Chisomo picked the dog book. Her friend Esnart found a page with a word she had never seen before.

“Can you help me?” Esnart asked. Chisomo did not know the word either. They asked Mrs Banda, who sounded it out with them. The two friends tried again until they could say it themselves.

At the end of the lesson, the pupils returned the books carefully. They made a list so everyone would get a turn the next week.

“The best part was not finding a book I already understood,” Chisomo said. “It was discovering something new with a friend.”

Mrs Banda smiled. The bag was still small, but the ideas inside it had already grown.
