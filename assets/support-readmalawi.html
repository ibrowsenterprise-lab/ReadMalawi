What is ReadMalawi’s current status?

ReadMalawi is an independent reading initiative founded by Jones Nalikungwi. It is not registered as an NGO or charity. Its work so far centres on digital book sharing and online reading. Physical book collection and school or prison distributions are future activities, not completed achievements.

Does contributing change access to books?

No. Reading remains free. Contributions are voluntary and separate from any future paid services.

How will contributions be recorded?

ReadMalawi will keep a separate record of confirmed contributions, processing fees, withdrawals and spending. Public updates will summarise confirmed contributions, actual fees, spending and the remaining balance without publishing private donor details. We will report actual spending against the purposes described above; the website does not currently display a verified fundraising total. You may request an update at info@readmalawi.fyi.

Will my name be published?

ReadMalawi will not publish your name or message without your permission. The payment provider may need contact details to process your contribution.

Can I get a tax-deductible donation receipt?

ReadMalawi does not offer tax-deductible charitable receipts. Any payment acknowledgement records the contribution only.

What if I have a payment problem or request a refund?

Keep your transaction reference and contact ReadMalawi. Please do not include your PIN, full card number or identity documents. We will review the transaction and explain the available next steps.
