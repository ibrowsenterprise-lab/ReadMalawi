# ReadMalawi local-language translation workflow

Starting priority languages: English, Chichewa, Chitumbuka, Chiyao, Chilomwe, Chisena, Chitonga. Additional languages and dialects can be added in response to community needs and actual reviewer availability.

**Current status:** English site is complete; Chichewa sample is an AI-assisted draft requiring fluent human checking; no complete translations are certified.

## Workflow
1. Write or licence an original English passage. Confirm translation rights and attribution.
2. Record source, author, licence, word count, reading level, and dialect/region if relevant.
3. AI may propose a translation **as a draft**, never as published truth.
4. Primary reviewer fluent in target language checks accuracy, orthography, cultural nuance and reading level.
5. Second checker reviews changed passages when possible. Editor approves and records date/version.
6. Publish with translator and reviewer credit (with their permission), language tag, source, licence and correction contact.
7. Provide a visible mechanism to suggest corrections without revealing volunteers' private information.

### Review questions
- Is the meaning preserved, including negations and quantities?
- Does it sound natural for readers in Malawi? What dialect is used?
- Are names/proper nouns handled consistently?
- Is the content age-appropriate and safe to share?
- Has the rights holder licensed both reuse and translation?
- Are credit and accessible English fallback provided?
